## 📝 docs(release): 6.7.0 release prep — release-notes index and upgrade guide (2026-11-03)

**Repo:** EDDI (`docs/release-6.7.0-prep`)

### What changed and why

`docs/release-notes.md` stopped at 6.5.0, so 6.6.0 (tagged 2026-10-20) had no row and the 6.5 upgrade
guide was not listed under "Upgrade guides". It now has rows for 6.6.0 and 6.7.0 and lists the guide.
The 6.7.0 row is marked unreleased, to be set to the tag date at tagging.

`docs/upgrading-from-6.5.md` gains a "Changes in 6.7" section written on the assumption that the open
6.7 PRs (cluster hardening, team budgets, HITL review/oversight, cost accounting, the update check,
the review sweep) all merge: changed defaults, fail-closed behaviour, the cluster/NATS/Helm notes,
rolling-upgrade cautions and the opt-in feature switches.

### Design decisions

- No version files were touched: `pom.xml` already says 6.7.0 and the release pointers correctly
  stay on 6.6.0 until the post-release PR after the 6.7.0 image exists.
- The helm chart version and `operator-revision.json` are bumped by the PRs that change them, not
  here, to avoid re-conflicting the serially landing operator PRs.
- The upgrade section links only to pages that exist on main today; pages added by open PRs
  (e.g. group human decisions) are referenced through `group-conversations.md`.
