## 🐛 fix(tenancy): the cost gates fail closed on both datastores, and undo no longer refunds spend (2026-10-24)

**Repo:** EDDI (`fix/cost-gate-consistency`)

### What changed and why

Follow-ups to the tenant monthly cost budget and the per-conversation cost ceiling.

- **The pre-call cost gate answered a store outage differently per backend.** `PostgresTenantQuotaStore.getMonthlyCost` caught the `SQLException` and returned `0.0`, so the gate read "nothing spent" and allowed every model and tool call (fail open). `MongoTenantQuotaStore.getMonthlyCost` did not catch the `MongoException`, and `LlmTask` asks `LlmSpendGate` outside its guarded model phase, so the turn failed instead of serving the fallback answer. [`tenant-quotas.md`](../tenant-quotas.md) promised fail closed. Both stores now raise `QuotaAccountingUnavailableException`, as their `getQuota` already did ([`ITenantQuotaStore`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/tenancy/ITenantQuotaStore.java) documents the contract), and [`TenantQuotaService.checkCostBudget`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/tenancy/TenantQuotaService.java) turns it into a refusal flagged `accountingUnavailable`, counted on `eddi.tenant.quota.unavailable{type=cost}`. An exception that still escapes the check is a defect, not an outage. [`LlmSpendGate`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/modules/llm/impl/LlmSpendGate.java) and the tool-call gate in [`ToolLoopRunner`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/modules/llm/impl/ToolLoopRunner.java) both refuse it (the fallback answer, a tool error result) under a separate reason, `tenant_check_error`. It is counted on `eddi.llm.spend.refused` and logged at ERROR, with the stack trace at most once a minute. It does not pass for an outage on `eddi.tenant.quota.unavailable`.
- **say → undo → say discarded spend.** The ceiling summed `audit:cost` over the live steps. Undo moved the priced step to the redo cache, and the next say cleared it, so a public `/undo` reset what a conversation had spent. [`ConversationMemory.startNextStep()`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/memory/ConversationMemory.java) now carries the discarded steps' spend onto the new step as `audit:discarded_cost`, a new key in [`MemoryKeys`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/memory/MemoryKeys.java) that is persisted with the step. The ceiling counts `audit:discarded_cost` and the redo cache, which a rerun runs against. The snapshot's conversation total counts both as well, so it no longer drops between an undo and the next say; it is read by the Manager, the group cost ledger, Slack and schedule deltas. Redo leaves the total unchanged, and a rerun already added to the step's own `audit:cost`.
- **Undo and redo kept the wrong `audit:pending_cost`.** `TurnCostMeter` reads the owed tenant charge from the newest step that has one, and undo and redo reorder those steps. An undone step's pending charge was lost on the next say. A rerun after an undo re-charged an amount the undone step had already recovered. A rerun's own outage charge was then shadowed by the undone or redone step's older value. Undo and redo now copy the pending value of the step they move aside onto the step that becomes current, `0.0` included. That step always holds the newer of the two values, and both writes are full replaces, so the copy is persisted.
- **Dream charges lost to an outage** are carried in [`DreamService`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/runtime/internal/DreamService.java) (node-local, in-process) and added to the next priced consolidation charge. The docs say a restart loses it.
- **Manager quotas page.** The cost-budget status checked "unlimited" before "enforced", so a disabled tenant with no budget was told its spend was metered, although `recordCost` records nothing while enforcement is off. Enforcement is checked first, with a new `quotas.budgetDisabled` message in all 11 locales. Amounts under $1 keep up to four decimals, so $0.006 against a $0.01 cap no longer reads "$0.01 of $0.01 … $0.00 left".
- `TurnCostMeterTest`'s outage test claimed the marker is left untouched. It advances, as the class documents, and the test now says so and asserts it.
- `ReservedPrincipalsTest` (inherited from main) declared the `"sha256:"` prefix twice, once for brand image hashes and once for mail Message-IDs. `Map.ofEntries` rejects duplicate keys, so the class failed to initialise. The two entries are now one, whose description covers both uses.

### Decisions

- `LlmSpendGate.countMidLoop` stays on `Metrics.globalRegistry`. Its caller, `ToolLoopRunner`, is not a CDI bean and is built without a registry in `AgentOrchestrator` and a dozen tests. That is the same idiom as `LifecycleManager`, and Quarkus adds its registry to the global composite. The Javadoc now says so.
- The old-JSON round trip for `maxCostPerConversationUsd` already existed (`ConversationCostCeilingTest.storedConfigCompatibility`), so no test was added for it.
- Not added: an IT of the full deployment path, from a priced model through `ConversationService` to a refused tool call. The existing IT scaffolding has no priced model to drive through the service. The store-level halves are covered for both datastores in `TenantCostMeteringIT`: a monthly-cost read that fails at the gate, and say → undo → say persisted through the real conversation store.
