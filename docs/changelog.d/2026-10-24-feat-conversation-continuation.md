## ✨ feat(engine): self-continuation — an agent can start its own next turn (2026-10-24)

**Repo:** EDDI (`feat/conversation-continuation`) — item E8 (core) of the evaluated-generation plan

### What changed and why

A job of many model calls (the plan's video pipeline: ~150 calls over ~20 minutes) cannot be one turn: the per-request deadline header is capped at 10 minutes, and the turn is EDDI's unit of tracing, persistence and recovery. Instead of a workflow engine, a job is now many ordinary turns of one conversation, and the conversation is its job log.

- **`CONTINUE_CONVERSATION`** — a new reserved action ([`IConversation`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/lifecycle/IConversation.java), [`ReservedActionLint`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/hitl/lint/ReservedActionLint.java)). A turn that emits it queues the next turn of the same conversation, without user input, **after the turn has been committed**.
- **Off unless configured.** Two agent-level fields ([`AgentConfiguration`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/configs/agents/model/AgentConfiguration.java)): `maxContinuations` (per conversation, `0`..`10000`, default unset = off — the action is ignored with a WARN, the step records `continuation:refused = disabled`, and `eddi.continuation.refused{reason="disabled"}` counts it) and `continuationInput` (a literal string, ≤ 4096 characters, never a template; default empty). Validated at save time and on ZIP import ([`AgentStore`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/configs/agents/mongo/AgentStore.java)).
- **Bounds.** Each continuation is an ordinary turn under the agent's `turnDeadlineMs`. The count is the number of steps carrying the engine-written marker `continuation:number`. `maxCostPerConversationUsd` bounds them all together — it already sums `audit:cost` over every step (`LlmSpendGate.conversationCost`, verified); a continuation is also refused before it is armed once the ceiling is reached. The tenant budget applies through `say` like on any turn.
- **What a continuation turn sees.** The designer's `continuationInput` and the engine-reserved context entry `selfContinuation = {number, max}` (added to [`ClientContextGuard.RESERVED_KEYS`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/security/ClientContextGuard.java), so a client cannot forge it). Rules route on a phase property, so the job is a state machine in config.
- **Delivery: a one-shot schedule, not the coordinator queue** ([`ConversationContinuations`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/continuation/ConversationContinuations.java), [`ContinuationFireHandler`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/continuation/ContinuationFireHandler.java), a fast-path in [`ScheduleFireExecutor`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/runtime/internal/ScheduleFireExecutor.java)). The coordinator's queue lives in memory and dies with its node; a schedule row survives a restart, exactly one node claims it, a failed fire is retried with backoff and dead-lettered, and the turn it starts still goes through `say` — coordinator, conversation lease and fencing, quotas, audit. The cost is the poll interval (≤ 15 s) between two continuation turns.
- **Once only.** Delivery is at-least-once, so two guards: the fire first looks for a step carrying its number in the stored conversation (written before the persist, never expires), and the turn carries an automatic `Idempotency-Key` (`eddi-continuation-<n>`). Under the lease, a queued turn rebuilt over a newer document is dropped when that document already holds its marker — also with the `Idempotency-Key` feature switched off ([`ConversationService`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/internal/ConversationService.java)).
- **Lifecycle.** Decided before the persist (the outcome — `continuation:next` or `continuation:refused` — is part of the committed step) and armed only after it, in the say path ([`ConversationStepRunner`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/internal/ConversationStepRunner.java)), the resume path and the `CONVERSATION_START` turn. Nothing is armed for a turn that paused, failed, was cancelled, lost its lease or could not be stored. A fire never runs a turn for an `ENDED`, `AWAITING_HUMAN`, deleted/erased or Art. 18-restricted conversation; an undeployed agent is retried (undeploy disables the schedule, redeploy re-enables it) and dead-lettered; an interrupted continuation stops the chain. `endConversation` and `cancel` withdraw the pending continuation — cancel between two continuation turns now answers `CANCELLED` instead of `409` ([`ConversationHitlService`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/internal/ConversationHitlService.java)); the approver's paused-only cancel never touches it. A group member's turn cannot continue itself. The schedule carries the owner's `userId`, so erasure deletes it.
- **Schedule surfaces.** A continuation schedule cannot be fired manually (REST `409`, MCP error) and is never exported ([`RestScheduleStore`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/schedule/rest/RestScheduleStore.java), [`McpAdminTools`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/mcp/McpAdminTools.java), [`RestExportService`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/backup/impl/RestExportService.java)).
- **Metrics:** `eddi.continuation.scheduled`, `eddi.continuation.run`, `eddi.continuation.refused{reason}`.

### Not in this change (planned)

Part 2 of E8 — parking a turn on an external long-running operation (the HITL pause machinery with a machine as the resolver, a poll schedule bound to the `pauseId`, an optional signed webhook to resolve it sooner) — and the async video provider (needs E6/E7).

New: `ConversationContinuationsTest`, `ContinuationFireHandlerTest`, `ConversationServiceContinuationTest` (the real `say` path: armed only after the commit; fire twice → one pipeline run through the stored document; two deliveries serialized by the lease → one run, with and without `Idempotency-Key`), `AgentStoreContinuationValidationTest`; extended `ConversationHitlServiceCancelTest`, `ScheduleFireExecutorTest`, `RestScheduleStoreTest`, `AgentStoreClientLibraryMemoryToolsTest`, `ClientContextGuardTest`.

```decision-log
| 2026-10-24 | Self-continuation is delivered as a one-shot schedule on the cluster-aware schedule machinery, not through the conversation coordinator's queue | E8 needs a continuation that survives a node crash, runs once under the conversation lease, and lands in dead letters when lost | Coordinator queue (in memory, lost on a crash); a custom scheduler (AGENTS.md forbids it) |
| 2026-10-24 | `maxContinuations` counts per conversation (steps carrying the engine-written `continuation:number`), not per chain | A counter the author or a property setter can reset is no bound; a job that needs more starts a new conversation | A conversation property as counter (writable by config); per-chain counts reset by user input |
```
