## ✨ feat(engine): self-continuation — an agent can start its own next turn (2026-10-24)

**Repo:** EDDI (`feat/conversation-continuation`) — item E8 (core) of the evaluated-generation plan

### What changed and why

A job of many model calls (the plan's video pipeline: ~150 calls over ~20 minutes) cannot be one turn: the per-request deadline header is capped at 10 minutes, and the turn is EDDI's unit of tracing, persistence and recovery. Instead of a workflow engine, a job is now many ordinary turns of one conversation, and the conversation is its job log.

- **`CONTINUE_CONVERSATION`** — a new reserved action ([`IConversation`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/lifecycle/IConversation.java), [`ReservedActionLint`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/hitl/lint/ReservedActionLint.java)). A turn that emits it queues the next turn of the same conversation, without user input, **after the turn has been committed**.
- **Off unless configured.** Two agent-level fields ([`AgentConfiguration`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/configs/agents/model/AgentConfiguration.java)): `maxContinuations` (per conversation, `0`..`10000`, default unset = off — the action is ignored with a WARN, the step records `continuation:refused = disabled`, and `eddi.continuation.refused{reason="disabled"}` counts it) and `continuationInput` (a literal string, ≤ 4096 characters, never a template; default empty). Validated at save time and on ZIP import ([`AgentStore`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/configs/agents/mongo/AgentStore.java)).
- **Bounds.** Each continuation is an ordinary turn under the agent's `turnDeadlineMs`. The count is the number of steps carrying the engine-written marker `continuation:number`. `maxCostPerConversationUsd` bounds them all together — it already sums `audit:cost` over every step (`LlmSpendGate.conversationCost`, verified); a continuation is also refused before it is armed once the ceiling is reached. The tenant budget applies through `say` like on any turn.
- **What a continuation turn sees.** The designer's `continuationInput` and the engine-reserved context entry `selfContinuation = {number, max}` (added to [`ClientContextGuard.RESERVED_KEYS`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/security/ClientContextGuard.java), so a client cannot forge it). Rules route on a phase property, so the job is a state machine in config.
- **Delivery: a one-shot schedule, not the coordinator queue** ([`ConversationContinuations`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/continuation/ConversationContinuations.java), [`ContinuationFireHandler`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/continuation/ContinuationFireHandler.java), a fast-path in [`ScheduleFireExecutor`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/runtime/internal/ScheduleFireExecutor.java)). The coordinator's queue lives in memory and dies with its node; a schedule row survives a restart, exactly one node claims it, a failed fire is retried with backoff and dead-lettered, and the turn it starts still goes through `say` — coordinator, conversation lease and fencing, quotas, audit. The cost is the poll interval (≤ 15 s) between two continuation turns.
- **Once only.** Delivery is at-least-once, so two guards: the fire first looks for a step carrying its number in the stored conversation (written before the persist, never expires), and the turn carries an automatic `Idempotency-Key` (`eddi-continuation-<n>`). Under the lease, a queued turn rebuilt over a newer document is dropped when that document already holds its marker — also with the `Idempotency-Key` feature switched off ([`ConversationService`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/internal/ConversationService.java)).
- **Lifecycle.** Decided before the persist (the outcome — `continuation:next` or `continuation:refused` — is part of the committed step) and armed only after it, in the say path ([`ConversationStepRunner`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/internal/ConversationStepRunner.java)), the resume path and the `CONVERSATION_START` turn. Nothing is armed for a turn that paused, failed, was cancelled, lost its lease or could not be stored. A fire never runs a turn for an `ENDED`, `AWAITING_HUMAN`, deleted/erased or Art. 18-restricted conversation; an undeployed agent is retried (undeploy disables the schedule, redeploy re-enables it) and dead-lettered; an interrupted continuation stops the chain. `endConversation` and `cancel` withdraw the pending continuation — cancel between two continuation turns now answers `CANCELLED` instead of `409` ([`ConversationHitlService`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/internal/ConversationHitlService.java)); the approver's paused-only cancel never touches it. A group member's turn cannot continue itself. The schedule carries the owner's `userId`, so erasure deletes it.
- **Schedule surfaces.** A continuation schedule cannot be fired manually (REST `409`, MCP error) and is never exported ([`RestScheduleStore`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/schedule/rest/RestScheduleStore.java), [`McpAdminTools`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/mcp/McpAdminTools.java), [`RestExportService`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/backup/impl/RestExportService.java)).
- **Metrics:** `eddi.continuation.scheduled`, `eddi.continuation.run`, `eddi.continuation.refused{reason}` — described in [`metrics.md`](../metrics.md#self-continuation-metrics) and charted in the Scheduling row of the Full Metrics Reference (k8s copy re-synced).
- **Docs:** new page [Long Jobs — the Conversation as the Job Log](../long-jobs.md); reserved-action lists ([`agent-config-authoring.md`](../agent-config-authoring.md), [`hitl.md`](../hitl.md), `AGENTS.md`), reserved context key ([`passing-context-information.md`](../passing-context-information.md), [`configuration-reference.md`](../configuration-reference.md)), [`conversations.md`](../conversations.md), [`scheduling.md`](../scheduling.md) (engine-minted schedules), [`httpcalls.md`](../httpcalls.md) (do not poll long operations with retries), [`upgrading-from-6.5.md`](../upgrading-from-6.5.md) (newly reserved names, the cancel `200`).
- **Manager:** a **Self-continuation** section on the agent page beside Limits (`maxContinuations` with the backend's 0..10000 bound, refusing to save an invalid count; `continuationInput`), i18n in all 11 locales ([`agent-config-sections.tsx`](../../ui/manager/src/components/editors/agent-config-sections.tsx), [`continuation.ts`](../../ui/manager/src/lib/continuation.ts)).
- **Platform Operator:** the prompt explains self-continuation, its schedule, step records and metrics, and maps the `long-jobs` page; revision **36 → 37**. Other open branches also bump `operator-revision.json` — operator PRs land one at a time.

### Review fixes

- **Only the engine arms a continuation.** REST schedule create, update and ZIP import accepted the `continuationType` marker, and a fire runs a turn in the conversation its metadata names — so a client that may create schedules could inject a turn into another user's conversation. The marker is now refused (`400`, for everyone, like the HITL-timeout and ingestion markers), and a fire whose schedule `userId` does not own the conversation runs nothing (`owner_mismatch`, a new refusal reason) ([`RestScheduleStore`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/schedule/rest/RestScheduleStore.java), [`ContinuationFireHandler`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/continuation/ContinuationFireHandler.java)).
- **A fire is `COMPLETED` only for a turn that was stored.** The reply reaches the handler before the persist; the handler now confirms the turn's marker is in the stored conversation (re-checked for about two seconds) and fails the fire otherwise, so the schedule retries it.
- **A schedule write that fails for a moment is retried** (three attempts) before `schedule_failed`. The crash window between commit and arm is documented in [long-jobs.md](../long-jobs.md) with how to spot and resume a stopped job; an automatic reconciliation sweep is planned with part 2.
- Manager: a half-typed count (`5e`, `-`) no longer silently clears `maxContinuations` and lets Save through.
- Docs: only continuation turns are retried by their schedule (the first turn is resent by its caller); the poll interval does not guarantee a minimum delay between continuations.
- **A cancel that races the arm wins.** A cancel that signalled a running turn found no schedule to withdraw yet, and the turn then armed one. Arming now skips a cancelled turn, and withdraws a schedule it wrote if the cancel landed during the write (`interrupted`).
- **Interrupts are kept.** An interrupted retry pause gives up and keeps the thread's interrupt flag, and the continuation fire log is written with the interrupt parked and restored afterwards, as the other fire paths already do.

- **End-to-end ITs on both datastores** ([`ConversationContinuationIT`](../../src/test/java/ai/labs/eddi/integration/ConversationContinuationIT.java) and its Postgres twin, with a one-second poll profile). A deterministic agent (rules + output, no LLM) runs one user turn, then the real poller runs continuations 1..3 on its own and the fourth is refused (`limit`); asserted on the stored conversation through REST (markers, `continuation:next`, the reserved context, the literal input), the schedule listing and the fire logs. Also: cancel between turns withdraws the pending continuation (`200`) and nothing runs after it, `endConversation` withdraws it, REST create/update with the marker is `400`, and an agent without `maxContinuations` arms nothing.
- **Fixed, found by those ITs: cancelling a finished job answered `CANCELLED`.** A fired one-shot is disabled, not deleted, so every continuation that ran stays under `continuation-<conversationId>`, and `disarm` counted those spent rows as withdrawn: a cancel after the job ended answered `200` instead of `409`. `disarm` still deletes them but now reports only the pending ones — enabled, or switched off by an undeploy — through a new `IScheduleStore.countPendingSchedulesByName` (Mongo and Postgres) ([`ConversationContinuations`](../../src/main/java/ai/labs/eddi/engine/continuation/ConversationContinuations.java)).

- **Spent continuation rows are bounded.** Every fired continuation stayed behind as a disabled one-shot with its fire log — up to `maxContinuations` rows per conversation. Arming the next continuation, and the last continuation turn of a job, now first deletes the conversation's used-up rows with their fire logs through a new `IScheduleStore.deleteUsedUpOneShotsByName` (Mongo and Postgres, reusing the delete cascades). Used up means exactly what `markCompleted` leaves for a one-shot: disabled, `PENDING`, no next fire, fired, unclaimed, no disabled reason — so the running continuation's claimed row, dead-lettered, undeploy-disabled and hand-disabled rows are never touched, and a conversation keeps at most its latest used-up continuation plus the pending one. A failed prune is logged and never stops the arm. Rejected: deleting a continuation's row when its fire completes, which would erase its fire history and put continuation logic into the generic poller. `deleteSchedulesByName` no longer logs every deletion as "HITL timeout schedule(s)".
